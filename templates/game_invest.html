Investment Basics for Teens
Low Risk Options:
  • Savings Account: Money is guaranteed safe
  • CDs: Higher interest but money is locked up
  • Government Bonds: Very safe, moderate returns
Higher Risk Options:
  • Index Funds: Diversified, moderate risk
  • Individual Stocks: Can be very volatile
  • Crypto: Extremely risky and unpredictable
Key Lessons:
  • Time Matters: The longer you invest, the more your money can grow
  • Risk vs Reward: Higher potential returns usually mean higher risk
  • Diversification: Don't put all your eggs in one basket
  • Start Early: Even small amounts can grow significantly over time
